Premier League Rivalries

Eight clubs have stayed in the Premier League every one of the last 10 seasons (2016–17 through 2025–26) without being relegated: Arsenal, Chelsea, Crystal Palace, Everton, Liverpool, Manchester City, Manchester United and Tottenham Hotspur. For each, here are their fans' five most hated rivals, rated 1 (mild needle) to 5 (blood feud).
