The 34-club list is every team that played at least one Premier League match in the last 10 seasons (2016–17 through 2025–26), derived from season-by-season promotion/relegation results — from ever-present sides (Arsenal, Chelsea, Crystal Palace, Everton, Liverpool, Manchester City, Manchester United, Tottenham Hotspur, West Ham) down to single-season visitors (Cardiff, Hull, Ipswich, Luton, Middlesbrough). Rivalry selections and intensity ratings are a synthesis of long-running fan-rivalry surveys (including the widely cited "League of Love and Hate" fan census) and documented club/derby history — treat the 1–5 scores as an editorial estimate of intensity, not an exact measurement. Some cited rivals (e.g. Millwall, Leeds' and Sheffield United's Yorkshire neighbours, Birmingham City, Portsmouth) haven't played Premier League football in the last 10 seasons; they're included as rivalry targets only, so their own outgoing rivalries aren't charted here.
